our story

One day, all we’ll have are the stories. So let’s make them worth telling.

MICHELLE & JONATHAN

Dos Culturas Wander started with an idea we kept talking about but never really got around to doing. We wanted to create a space where we could share our travels, the places we discovered, the food we tried, and everything we experienced along the way. When we finally had the opportunity to start traveling together, we thought, why not now?

The name came naturally. We're two people from different cultures, Filipino and Mexican, with different backgrounds, personalities, and ways of seeing the world. Even when we're standing in the same place, looking at the same thing, we can walk away with completely different impressions. That's something we've always found interesting about traveling together.

Jonathan loves exploring as much as possible. He's fascinated by architecture, scenery, traditions, and the little things that reveal how people live. Michelle sometimes prefers taking her time, noticing details, finding beautiful corners, and capturing moments that might otherwise pass unnoticed. We don't always agree on what to do first or where to go next. Sometimes one of us has an entire itinerary in mind while the other wants to stop somewhere unexpected. But we make room for both. We try the things each other loves, discover things neither of us expected, and somehow find our own rhythm along the way.

Photography and filmmaking were already part of Michelle's life before DCW. At first, bringing a camera along was simply a way to remember our trips and share them with others. Over time, we started paying more attention to how we captured the places we stayed, the meals we enjoyed, and the experiences we wanted other people to discover. We realized that the same photographs and films we loved creating could also help businesses show what makes their places and experiences special.

That's how our travel journal gradually grew into something more.

Working with businesses has brought a different side to our travels. Sometimes it means planning a shoot before we can simply enjoy a place. Sometimes an idea that looked perfect on paper doesn't work once we arrive, and something completely unexpected turns out better. Every property, restaurant, and experience has its own character, so we don't believe in approaching every project exactly the same way.

We listen to what our clients want and make sure their expectations come first, but we also enjoy bringing our own ideas into the process. Two people can look at the same space and notice completely different possibilities. That's one of the things we value about working together. We challenge each other's perspectives, experiment, adjust, and try to create something that feels right for the place we're capturing.

And nothing compares to seeing the finished work and knowing the people we created it for are happy with the result. Looking back at our earliest projects and seeing how much we've learned along the way is something we're genuinely proud of.

Still, we never want to lose the reason we started.

We're a regular couple. We have our disagreements, our spontaneous decisions, our plans that don't always work out, and plenty of moments when we just want to put the camera away and enjoy being somewhere new. We love trying unfamiliar food, experiencing different traditions, discovering places we didn't know existed, and sharing those moments with each other.

We also believe that sharing an experience means being truthful about it. Collaborations are an exciting opportunity, but they don't change our opinions. We want people to trust that when we recommend somewhere, it's because we genuinely enjoyed it, not simply because we were invited.

More than anything, we want our photographs, films, and stories to make people feel like they're there with us. To see a place, a meal, or an experience and think, I want to go there. I want to try that.

We don't know exactly where Dos Culturas Wander will take us. We hope it continues opening doors to new places, new people, and creative opportunities. But years from now, what we'd love most is to look back at everything we've experienced together, remember the stories behind each photograph, and maybe one day share those memories with our children.

For us, DCW is our way of getting to know the world, together.
